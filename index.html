Empowering Young Minds Through Technology

Disruptors is a hands-on technology bootcamp created for young innovators aged 10 to 17. Students gain practical experience in coding, web development, mobile app development, robotics, digital creativity and problem solving through engaging projects and mentorship. Participants don't just watch—they build, design, code and solve real-world problems under experienced instructors.

Throughout the programme, students collaborate, create projects, develop confidence and acquire practical digital skills that extend far beyond the classroom.
